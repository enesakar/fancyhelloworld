    What to review

    • The title is the city, with where you are underneath. It shrinks into the top bar when you scroll.
    • Near is sorted by distance. Popular ranks places in your area by Wikipedia page views, so it shows rank numbers.
    • Each place shows its guide lengths: the 1-minute intro, then the length of Tell me more. Pasquino shows a place whose Wikipedia article is too short for more than an intro.
    • The free counter sits under the switch. Playing a new place uses one guide and replaying doesn't. Keep tapping new places to watch it run out.
    • Once all 10 are used, places you haven't heard show a lock. The ones you've heard stay playable.
    • The player bar floats above the tab bar, as in Apple Music.
    • Proposed: a Listened tab with the places you've heard. Keep it or drop it.
    • Photos are drawn placeholders. The app will use each place's photo from Wikipedia.
